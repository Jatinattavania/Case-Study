Project Summary
 Medi Spa Las Vegas provides a range of cosmetic and wellness treatments—like Botox, Semaglutide, IV therapy, and Microneedling—to help clients achieve youthful, aesthetic, and healthy body transformations. The goal was to design a visually appealing, informative, and conversion-focused interface that feels as luxurious and calming as a spa session.

Industry Understanding
 Medical Spa (Medi Spa) sits at the intersection of healthcare and luxury wellness. Unlike typical salons, medi spas require greater trust, clinical clarity, and emotional reassurance—all while providing a smooth digital journey to treatments.

Visual Strategy
 Moodboard & Style
  Colors: Soft neutrals, warm beige tones, and light creams create a luxurious and trustworthy feel.
  Typography: Serif + Sans-serif pairing for elegance and readability.
  Imagery: Clean, editorial-style photography showcasing glowing skin, relaxed clients, and procedures.

UX Goals
 Educate users on treatments
 Build trust through visuals and testimonials
 Encourage bookings via a streamlined flow

Key Screens with Highlights
 Homepage
  Bold headline and CTA
  Subtle animations
  Calming color palette
  Hero section featuring Botox and weight loss solutions

Treatments Page
 Grid layout for treatments like Botox, Semaglutide, IV therapy, etc.
 Hover effects to keep user engagement
 Includes short descriptions to reduce bounce

Booking Page
 Clean form layout with step-by-step booking
 Mobile-first responsive layout
 Smart calendar for slot selection

Before & After Section
 Builds trust and credibility
 Real patient stories and images
 Subtle hover animation on photo reveal

Mobile Experience
 Fully responsive
 Sticky “Book Now” button
 Tap-friendly cards and forms

Outcomes
 Increased user time on site due to interactive layout
 Boosted conversion potential with simplified CTAs
 Enhanced user trust with visual storytelling

Final Thoughts
 Designing for a medi spa isn’t just about aesthetics—it’s about emotional reassurance, credibility, and ease. This project blends wellness and UX best practices to deliver a serene and highly functional user experience.
